# 📊 Procurement Dashboard  

A Tableau-based **Procurement Analytics Dashboard**, replicated from Qlik, that provides a **unified view of purchase orders, supplier performance, spend analysis, and procurement efficiency**.  

---

## 🎯 Objective  
Enable procurement teams and management to:  
- Monitor **spend, supplier reliability, and delivery performance**  
- Identify **high-cost categories and underperforming suppliers**  
- Track **defective rates, return rates, and supplier ratings**  
- Support **data-driven vendor management and cost optimization**  

---

## 📂 Dataset  
- **Purchase Orders Data** – order ID, supplier, procurement category, spend, cycle time  
- **Supplier Performance** – delivery timeliness, fill rate, defective %, return rate  
- **Regional Spend** – procurement spend split across regions  
- **Supplier Ratings** – quality, price, delivery, support, overall rating  

> **Grain:** Purchase Order & Supplier-level records  

---

## 🔑 KPIs  
- 🧾 **Total Purchase Orders:** 2,000  
- ⏱️ **Avg Cycle Time:** 17 days  
- 💰 **Total Spend:** 7.0M  
- 📦 **Supplier Fill Rate:** 98%  
- 🚚 **On-Time Delivery:** 7%  
- ❌ **Defective %:** 2%  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Sections  

### 1) **Cycle Time Analysis**  
- Trend of **average cycle time** (days) across months  

### 2) **Supplier Performance**  
- **On-Time Delivery % by Supplier**  
- **Supplier Fill Rate %**  
- **Return Rate %**  
- **Defective % by Supplier (donut chart)**  

### 3) **Spend Analysis**  
- **Spend by Procurement Category** (pie chart)  
- **Spend by Supplier** (treemap)  
- **Spend by Region** (table)  

### 4) **Supplier Ratings**  
- Tabular view of **supplier ratings** (Quality, Price, Delivery, Support, Overall)  

---

## ✨ Interactivity & Features  
- **Filters:** Month-Year, Procurement Category, Region  
- **Drilldowns:** Category → Supplier → Region level analysis  
- **Export:** CSV/Excel/PDF download (if enabled in Tableau)  
- **Tooltips:** Detailed supplier metrics on hover  

---

## ✅ Outcome / Impact  
- Improved **visibility into supplier performance** and cost drivers  
- Identified **procurement inefficiencies** (long cycle times, defective % trends)  
- Supported **better vendor negotiations** with supplier-level scorecards  
- Enhanced **regional cost monitoring** for budget allocation
