# 📊 Amazon Sales Dashboard

## 🎯 Objective
The purpose of this dashboard is to analyze Amazon sales performance across various categories, time periods, and regions.  
It helps track **sales quantity, revenue, shipment status, sales channels, and regional performance** to support decision-making and optimize operations.

---

## 📂 Dataset
- **Source**: Amazon sales transaction data (sample).
- **Size**: ~1,16,482 records.
- **Fields**: Date, Category, Size, Quantity, Amount, Courier Status, Sales Channel, B2B Indicator, Region/State.
- **Time Period**: March 2022 – June 2022.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Majority of orders were shipped successfully (**94.21%**).
- High sales contribution from **Amazon.in** (99.86% of total).
- Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu are the **top-performing states** in terms of sales quantity.
- Consistent weekly sales trend with peaks in early April and mid-May.
- B2B orders are very minimal (**0.72%**) compared to B2C.
- Category sales vary significantly by product **Size (M, L, XL, XXL, etc.)**.

---

## 📈 KPIs and Metrics
- **Total Quantity**: 1,16,482  
- **Total Amount**: ₹78,592,678  
- **Total Categories**: 9  
- **Total Sizes**: 11  
- **Total Products**: 7,153  

---

## 📊 Charts and Visuals Used
- 📉 **Line Chart** → Amount by Week & Category  
- 📊 **Stacked Bar Chart** → Quantity by Week & Category, Quantity by Size & Category  
- 🥧 **Donut Chart** → Quantity by Courier Status & Category  
- 📊 **Horizontal Bar Chart** → Top 10 States by Quantity, Quantity by Status  
- 🗺️ **Map Visualization** → Quantity by State  

---

## ✨ Features
- Interactive filters for **Category & Time Period**.
- Drill-down by **Region, Size, and Courier Status**.
- KPIs displayed at the top for quick snapshot.
- Geographic insights with an **India Map**.
- Breakdown of shipment statuses (delivered, cancelled, in transit, etc.).

---

## ✅ Outcome / Impact
- Provides **end-to-end visibility** into sales and shipment performance.  
- Helps identify **top-performing categories and states**.  
- Assists in tracking **delivery efficiency** and reducing cancellations/returns.  
- Enables management to **plan inventory and logistics** more effectively.
