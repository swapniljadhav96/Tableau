# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard  

## 🎯 Objective  
This dashboard focuses on **analyzing customer churn behavior, revenue trends, and subscription lifecycle**.  
It provides visibility into **new vs recurring customers, churn rates, refunds, upgrades, and resurrections**.  
The goal is to help businesses **reduce churn, improve retention, and maximize customer lifetime value (CLV).**  

---

## 📂 Dataset  
- **Source**: Subscription and transaction data from Jan 2021 – Oct 2022.  
- **Key Fields**: Customer ID, Subscription Type, Revenue, Refunds, Country, Upgrade Date, Resurrection Date, Cohorts.  
- **Data Size**: Thousands of transaction records covering multiple geographies.  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights  
- **Churn Rates**:  
  - Overall churn fluctuated between **1.5% – 7.7%** in 2022.  
  - Lowest churn in **July 2022 (3.1%)**, highest in **Jan 2022 (7.7%)**.  

- **Revenue Breakdown**:  
  - **Net Revenue** peaked at **$55.7K in Jan 2022** but fluctuated due to refunds.  
  - Strong revenue contribution from **recurring customers (70–80%)** compared to new customers.  

- **Customer Types**:  
  - New vs Recurring → Recurring consistently dominates with **75%+ share**.  
  - Average Order Value (AOV): ~28.8.  

- **Geography**:  
  - Highest revenue from **US, GB, CA, IN, DE**.  
  - Emerging contributions from APAC regions.  

- **Retention & Resurrections**:  
  - 291 users were **resurrected** with an average resurrection time of ~146 days.  
  - Monthly cohorts show retention declining over time, with sharp drop-offs after **Period 2–3**.  

- **Upgrades**:  
  - 235 users upgraded **annual → annual**, 107 upgraded **monthly → monthly**, showing loyalty among certain cohorts.  

---

## 📈 KPIs and Metrics  
- 📉 **Churn Rate (%)**  
- 💰 **Net Revenue vs Refunds**  
- 👥 **New vs Recurring Customers**  
- 🌍 **Revenue by Country**  
- 🔄 **User Upgrades**  
- 🔁 **User Resurrections**  
- 📊 **Cohort Retention (Monthly & Annual)**  

---

## 📊 Charts and Visuals Used  
- 📈 **Line Chart** → Net Revenue vs Refunds by Month  
- 📉 **Line Chart** → Churn Rate Trends  
- 📊 **Stacked Bar Chart** → New vs Recurring Customers per Month  
- 🌍 **Geo Map** → Revenue by Country  
- ⚪ **Bubble Chart** → User Upgrades & Resurrections  
- 📊 **Cohort Matrix** → Retention Rates by Month (0–8 periods)  

---

## ✨ Features  
- Interactive filters for **Subscription Type (Monthly, Annual, Lifetime)**.  
- Cohort analysis for **retention tracking**.  
- Drill-down by **Country** and **Customer Segment**.  
- Comparative view of **Upgrades vs Resurrections**.  

---

## ✅ Outcome / Impact  
- Helped identify **critical churn periods** where retention strategies should focus (within first 3 months).  
- Revealed **high-value geographies** (US, UK, Canada, India) for customer targeting.  
- Provided insights to optimize **pricing models & subscription upgrades**.  
- Informed retention campaigns that could **reduce churn by 2–3%**.
