# ✈️ Cross Domain Dashboard  

A Tableau-based **Airline Maintenance & Inventory Dashboard**, replicated from Qlik, providing a **360° view of Total Cost per Part (TCO), procurement costs, inventory efficiency, and budget utilization**.  

---

## 🎯 Objective  
Enable airline operations and finance teams to:  
- Monitor **aircraft maintenance costs** across fleet types  
- Track **inventory turnover, write-off %, and delivery performance**  
- Compare **budget vs actual write-off trends by region**  
- Support **data-driven cost control and fleet maintenance planning**  

---

## 📂 Dataset  
- **Aircraft Data** – aircraft ID, aircraft type, region, category  
- **Maintenance Costs** – per part, by aircraft, by category  
- **Inventory Metrics** – turnover rate, write-off %, inventory-to-sales ratio  
- **Budget Data** – actual budget, write-off vs budget by region  
- **Procurement Trends** – average procurement costs across months  

> **Grain:** Aircraft-level + Category-level records  
> **Period:** Monthly snapshot (2024 dataset)

## 📊 Dashboard Sections  

### 1) **Maintenance Cost Analysis**  
- Avg maintenance cost by **aircraft type**  
- Maintenance cost by **category & aircraft ID**  

### 2) **Inventory & Write-Off Metrics**  
- Inventory-to-sales ratio (gauge)  
- Write-off % vs budget (gauge)  
- Regional breakdown: **Budget vs Write-Off** (bar chart)  

### 3) **Procurement Cost Trend**  
- Monthly trend of **average procurement cost**  

### 4) **Summary Section**  
- Key metrics: total parts, avg maintenance cost, total budget  

---

## ✨ Interactivity & Features  
- **Filters:** Region, Month-Year, Aircraft Type, Category  
- **Drilldowns:** Aircraft Type → Category → Aircraft ID  
- **Export:** CSV/Excel/PDF (if enabled in Tableau)  
- **Tooltips:** Extra cost and budget details on hover

- Improved **visibility into fleet maintenance costs**
