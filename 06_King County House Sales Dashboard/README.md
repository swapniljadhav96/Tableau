# 🏡 King County, Washington House Sales Dashboard  

## 🎯 Objective  
The purpose of this dashboard is to analyze **real estate sales trends** in King County, Washington.  
It provides insights into **house prices, conditions, views, locations, and property characteristics** to better understand market behavior and housing demand.  

---

## 📂 Dataset  
- **Source**: King County House Sales dataset (Kaggle / public data).  
- **Period Covered**: 2015 (sample month – March).  
- **Key Fields**: Price, Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Sqft Living, Sqft Lot, Year Built, Condition, View, Zipcode, Sale Date.  
- **Size**: 21K+ records.  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights  
- **Daily Average Price**: Fluctuates between **350K – 650K** in March 2015.  
- **House Condition vs View**:  
  - Best conditions + excellent views fetch the **highest prices (~1M+)**.  
  - Poor/Fair condition homes sell much lower (~350K–500K).  
- **Price Distribution**:  
  - Majority of houses sold in the **250K – 600K** range.  
  - Few high-value properties exceed **1.2M+**.  
- **Bedrooms & Bathrooms**:  
  - 3–4 bedrooms most common.  
  - Most houses have 2–3 bathrooms.  
- **Geographic Spread**:  
  - High concentration of sales in **Seattle metro area** and nearby cities.  

---

## 📈 KPIs and Metrics  
- 💰 **Average Sale Price** (Daily & Monthly)  
- 🏡 **Distribution of Prices**  
- 🛏️ **Distribution of Bedrooms**  
- 🛁 **Distribution of Bathrooms**  
- 🌄 **View vs Condition Price Matrix**  
- 🗺️ **Map of House Sales by Zipcode**  

---

## 📊 Charts and Visuals Used  
- 📈 **Line Chart** → Daily average house price sales  
- 🌍 **Map** → House sales by region (King County)  
- 🟧 **Heatmap** → View vs Condition with average prices  
- 📊 **Histogram** → Distribution of house prices  
- 📊 **Histogram** → Bedrooms & Bathrooms distribution  
- 📅 **Calendar Heatmap** → Sales activity by day (March 2015)  

---

## ✨ Features  
- Interactive filters: **Year Built, Sqft Living, Sqft Lot, Date selection**.  
- Heatmap analysis for **condition vs view pricing impact**.  
- Drill-down on **geographical sales distribution**.  

---

## ✅ Outcome / Impact  
- Provided insights into **pricing patterns** based on house features.  
- Helped identify **high-demand zones** in King County (Seattle region).  
- Showed the **premium impact of good view & condition** on prices.  
- Useful for **real estate investors, agents, and buyers** to assess housing trends.
