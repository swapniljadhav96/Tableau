# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

A unified view of **Workforce, Engagement, Recruitment, and Learning & Development (L&D)** that provides actionable insights into employee trends, retention, hiring efficiency, and training effectiveness.

---

## 🎯 Objective
Provide HR leaders and managers with a **360° view of the workforce** to:
- Monitor **attrition vs retention**, engagement, hiring pipeline, and training outcomes
- Identify **risk areas** (high churn cohorts, low engagement teams, costly trainings)
- Enable **data-driven workforce planning** and policy decisions

---

## 📂 Dataset
- [Employee/HR Dataset (All in One)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ravindrasinghrana/employeedataset)

> **Period:** multi-month snapshot (aggregate)  
> **Grain:** employee-level + application-level + training record-level

---

## 🔑 KPIs (from current view)
- 👥 **Total Employees:** **2,401**  
- ↩️ **Attrition:** **934**  
- 🔒 **Retention Rate:** **60.3%**  
- 🕒 **Avg Tenure:** **3.2 years**  
- 🎂 **Avg Age:** **53.6 years**  
- ⭐ **Engagement Score:** **59.6%**  
- 🧑‍💼 **Recruitment Offered Rate:** **19.7%**  
- 🏷️ **Training Cost (Total):** **1.34M**  
- 💳 **Avg Cost/Employee:** **556.4**  
- 🎓 **Training Pass Rate (overall):** **25.2%**

---

## 📊 Visuals & Sections
### 1) Workforce Overview
- Headcount by **department / gender / age band / status**
- **Attrition vs Retention** trend
- Performance distribution by age groups

### 2) Engagement
- **Engagement, Satisfaction, Work–Life** scores (trend + distribution)
- **Engagement by Country / Department**
- At-risk segments with low engagement

### 3) Recruitment
- Monthly **Applications vs Offers** trend
- **Offer rate / rejection rate** by role & qualification
- **Salary expectations** distribution

### 4) Learning & Development
- **Training cost, duration, pass rate** by program & trainer
- **Internal vs External** training mix
- Program performance (e.g., **Technical, Leadership, PM**)

---

## 🧠 Key Insights
- Attrition is **elevated (934)** → focus on **retention programs** for high-risk cohorts  
- Engagement score **59.6%** indicates **room to improve** satisfaction & WLB  
- Recruitment efficiency (**19.7% offers**) shows **pipeline friction**  
- High training spend (**1.34M**) but **25.2% pass rate** → optimize programs & trainers  
- Age distribution skewed **older** → plan **succession & upskilling**

---

## ✨ Interactivity & Features
- Global filters: **Department, Country, Gender, Age band, Time**
- Drill-downs from **org → department → team → employee cohort**
- Tooltips with **cohort KPIs** and benchmark lines
- Download **CSV/PNG** of selected views (if enabled in BI tool)

---

## ✅ Outcome / Impact
- Centralized **people analytics** for leadership reviews
- Identified **churn drivers** and **low-engagement pockets**
- Prioritized **recruitment fixes** (screening, comp bands, funnel steps)
- Optimized **L&D** by reallocating budget to **high-ROI programs**
