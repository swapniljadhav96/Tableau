# 🏥 Risky Business: Insurance Insights Dashboard  

## 🎯 Objective  
The dashboard analyzes **health insurance charges** to understand how different factors like **region, smoking status, BMI, age, and gender** impact medical costs.  
It highlights the **risk patterns** and **cost drivers** that influence insurance pricing.  

---

## 📂 Dataset  
- **Source**: Insurance Charges Dataset (Kaggle / public dataset).  
- **Records**: ~1,338 entries.  
- **Fields**: Age, Gender, BMI, Smoking Status, Region, Charges.  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights  
- **Total Charges**: \$17.7M across all policyholders.  
- **Average Charges**: \$13,270.  
- **Smoking Impact**: Smokers make up ~20.5% of customers but account for **much higher charges** compared to non-smokers.  
- **Regional Differences**:  
  - Southeast region has the **highest average charges (~15K)**.  
  - Northwest and Southwest regions have **lower average charges (~11K–12K)**.  
- **Gender Split**: Almost equal distribution → 662 female vs 676 male customers.  
- **BMI Influence**: Higher BMI correlates with **increased charges**, especially among smokers.  

---

## 📈 KPIs and Metrics  
- 💰 **Total Charges**: \$17,755,825  
- 📊 **Average Charges per Person**: \$13,270

---

## 📊 Charts and Visuals Used  
- 📊 **Bar Chart** → Average charges by region  
- 📊 **Histogram** → Distribution of charges by age group  
- 🔵 **Scatter Plot** → BMI vs Charges (colored by smoker/non-smoker)  
- 📊 **Donut Chart** → Gender distribution  
- 📊 **Bar Chart** → Average charges by smoking status  

---

## ✨ Features  
- Region, Age, and Gender filters for interactive exploration.  
- Visual correlation between **lifestyle factors (BMI, smoking)** and charges.  
- Quick gender-based demographic snapshot.  

---

## ✅ Outcome / Impact  
- Showcases the **significant cost impact of smoking** on insurance premiums.  
- Provides regional insights for **pricing strategy**.  
- Demonstrates BMI as a **strong driver of higher medical charges**.  
- Can be used by insurance companies for **risk profiling** and policy decisions.
