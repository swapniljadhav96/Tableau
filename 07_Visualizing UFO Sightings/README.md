# 👽 Eyes on the Skies: Visualizing UFO Sightings  

## 🎯 Objective  
This dashboard explores **UFO sightings worldwide**, focusing on their **location, time trends, duration, and common shapes**.  
It provides a **visual storytelling experience** for analyzing how sightings are distributed across regions and years.  

---

## 📂 Dataset  
- **Source**: UFO Sightings dataset (NUFORC / Kaggle).  
- **Period Covered**: Year 2006 (sample shown).  
- **Key Fields**: Country, Date, Year, Duration, Location, UFO Shape, Sightings Count.  
- **Total Sightings (2006)**: 3,721 recorded events.  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights  
- **Geography**:  
  - Majority of sightings are concentrated in the **United States**.  
  - Some sightings reported in Canada, Mexico, and isolated global locations.  

- **Sightings Over Time**:  
  - Fluctuations throughout 2006 with **periodic spikes**.  
  - Some months reported **600+ sightings**.  

- **Duration**:  
  - Most sightings last **under 3 minutes**.  
  - Very few extended-duration reports.  

- **Shapes**:  
  - **Light formations** were the most reported shape (800+ sightings).  
  - Other common shapes: **circle, fireball, cigar, sphere, triangle**.  

---

## 📈 KPIs and Metrics  
- 🛸 **Total UFO Sightings Registered** – 3,721  
- ⏱️ **Duration Distribution** of sightings  
- 📊 **Sightings Over Time (trend analysis)**  
- 🌍 **Geographic Distribution by Country/Region**  
- 🔺 **Most Common UFO Shapes**  

---

## 📊 Charts and Visuals Used  
- 📈 **Line/Scatter Plot** → Sightings over time  
- 📊 **Bar Chart** → UFO duration distribution  
- 📊 **Bar Chart** → Common UFO shapes  
- 🌍 **Map** → Location of sightings (USA & worldwide)  

---

## ✨ Features  
- Dropdown filters: **Country, Year**.  
- Slider for **time-based analysis** (Year selection).  
- Interactive visuals showing **shape-wise and duration-based patterns**.  

---

## ✅ Outcome / Impact  
- Provides insights into **when and where UFO sightings are most frequent**.  
- Helps analyze **behavioral patterns** in reports (duration, shape, geography).  
- Offers a **storytelling visualization** for enthusiasts and researchers.
