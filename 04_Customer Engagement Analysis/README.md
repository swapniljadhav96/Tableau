# 📊 Customer Engagement Analysis Dashboard  

## 🎯 Objective  
This dashboard analyzes **student engagement, onboarding, exams, certifications, and subscription patterns** across online learning platforms.  
It helps track **course consumption, learner behavior, engagement cohorts, and certification outcomes** to optimize **learning experiences and retention strategies**.  

---

## 📂 Dataset  
- **Source**: Learning platform activity logs (Jan 2022 – Oct 2022).  
- **Records**: 19,332 engaged students, 3,683 certificates issued.  
- **Key Fields**: Student ID, Course Title, Engagement Minutes, Exam Attempts, Exam Outcomes, Subscription Type (Free/Paid), Registration Dates.  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights  
- **Engagement**:  
  - Students spent an average of **81.4 minutes per student**.  
  - Over **2.1M minutes watched** across all courses.  

- **Top Courses by Engagement**:  
  - *Introduction to Data & Data Science* – 297K mins watched  
  - *SQL* – 215K mins  
  - *Statistics* – 199K mins  
  - *Introduction to Excel* – 194K mins  
  - *Python Programmer Bootcamp* – 150K mins  

- **Course Ratings**:  
  - Average rating **4.79/5** (2,289 five-star ratings).  

- **Exams & Certifications**:  
  - Pass rates ranged from **65% – 74%** monthly.  
  - 3,683 certificates issued.  
  - Career tracks:  
    - 7,874 students enrolled,  
    - 928 attempted a course exam,  
    - 856 completed a course exam,  
    - 74 attempted a final exam,  
    - 43 earned a **career track certificate**.  

- **Cohorts**:  
  - Engagement varies by month, with higher consistency among **paid students**.  

- **Subscription & Learning**:  
  - Paid learners contributed significantly to **sustained engagement**.  
  - Average engagement per student peaked at **71 minutes** in some months.

- 👩‍🎓 **Engaged Students** – 19,332

- 🎓 **Certificates Issued** – 3,683  
- 📊 **Top Courses by Engagement**  
- ⭐ **Average Course Rating** – 4.79  
- 📝 **Exam Pass % vs Fail %**  
- 📈 **Cohort Engagement Trends**  
- 💳 **Paid vs Free Engagement**  

---

## 📊 Charts and Visuals Used  
- 📉 **Line Charts** → Engagement & Onboarding trends (daily, monthly)  
- 📊 **Bar Charts** → Course minutes watched (Top 5, Bottom 5)  
- ⭐ **Rating Distribution** → 5-star, 4-star, 3-star reviews  
- 📊 **Stacked Bar / Funnel** → Career track funnel (enrolled → certified)  
- 🥧 **Pie Charts** → Exam outcomes (Pass vs Fail %)  
- 📊 **Cohort Analysis** → Engagement over time by cohorts  

---

## ✨ Features  
- Interactive filters: **Free vs Paid, Time Period, Course Selection**.  
- Drill-down into **course-level engagement and completion**.  
- Cohort tracking to measure **retention & sustained learning**.  
- Exam & certificate funnel analysis.  

---

## ✅ Outcome / Impact  
- Helped identify **top-performing courses** driving the most engagement.  
- Revealed **engagement drop-offs** for targeted re-engagement strategies.  
- Enabled analysis of **exam performance** and **certificate conversion funnel**.  
- Supported decision-making for **course improvements, learner support, and marketing focus**.
