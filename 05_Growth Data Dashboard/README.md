# 📊 Growth Data Dashboard  

## 🎯 Objective  
The Growth Dashboard provides insights into **user acquisition, subscription growth, website traffic, and conversion funnels**.  
It helps track **new vs returning visitors, free-to-paid conversions, churn, resurrected users, and revenue growth across geographies and channels**.  

---

## 📂 Dataset  
- **Source**: Web analytics + subscription transaction logs.  
- **Period Covered**: July 2022 – Oct 2022.  
- **Key Fields**: Visitor ID, Session Date, Subscription Plan, Device, OS, Channel (UTM), Conversion Status, Funnel Stage.  
- **Subscriptions Analyzed**: Monthly, Quarterly, Annual, Lifetime.  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights  
- **Subscriptions**:  
  - Monthly: 297 (↓ 0.7% last 30 days).  
  - Quarterly: 55 (↓ 6.8%).  
  - Annual: 4,260 (↑ 1.0%).  

- **Free → Paid Conversion**:  
  - Conversion rates between **1.1% – 5.0%** weekly.  
  - Peak in early October with nearly **1,000 new free users**.  

- **Paid Users**:  
  - Mix of **direct vs converted** users.  
  - Converted users form a significant share of monthly growth.  

- **Churn & Resurrection**:  
  - Evident churn in both 30- and 90-day windows.  
  - Significant **resurrected users** indicating re-engagement potential.  

- **Website Traffic**:  
  - Weekly visitors ranged **28K – 43K** with fluctuations.  
  - Monthly visitors peaked at **154K (Oct 2022)**.  
  - Returning users contribute strongly to engagement.  

- **Geography**:  
  - Sessions mapped globally, with device distribution tracked per country.  
  - Paid conversions driven by channels like **Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, Organic search**.  

- **Funnel Analysis**:  
  - Visitor-to-paid funnel → **549,809 visitors → 16,281 free sign-ups → 8,836 watched content → 1,126 paid users**.  
  - Significant drop-offs at **free → watched** and **watched → paid** stages.  

---

## 📈 KPIs and Metrics  
- 👥 **Visitors (New vs Returning)**  
- 📊 **Conversion Rate (Free → Paid)**  
- 💳 **Subscriptions by Plan (Monthly, Quarterly, Annual, Lifetime)**  
- 📉 **Churn & Resurrections**  
- 🌍 **Sessions by Geography**  
- 🔄 **Visitor-to-Paid Funnel Performance**  

---

## 📊 Charts and Visuals Used  
- 📉 **Waterfall Charts** → Subscription growth (30, 90, 365 days)  
- 📊 **Bar Charts** → Free vs Paid users, Direct vs Converted sign-ups  
- 📈 **Line Charts** → Visitors over time (weekly & monthly)  
- 🌍 **Maps** → Sessions, Mobile usage, Paid conversions by country  
- 🔄 **Funnel Chart** → Visitor → Free → Watched → Paid journey  

---

## ✨ Features  
- Interactive date filters (weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly).  
- Drill-down by **subscription plan** (monthly, quarterly, annual).  
- Channel-based segmentation (Organic, Social Media, Paid Ads).  
- Visitor funnel breakdown for identifying drop-offs.  

---

## ✅ Outcome / Impact  
- Identified **low conversion stages** in the funnel → opportunity to improve onboarding & content engagement.  
- Showed **Annual plans growing steadily (+1.0%)** while Monthly & Quarterly dipped.  
- Provided **channel-level attribution** to optimize marketing spends (Facebook, YouTube, Organic strong).  
- Supported business in **strategic retention efforts** by highlighting churn & resurrected users.
